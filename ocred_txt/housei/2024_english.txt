I was stunned.
All I
had done was to briefly show UN data for Iran on the increase in life

I had also mentioned that

babies per woman nearly 15 years later.
The student said, “You stated that the fast fall in the number of babies
per woman in Iran is a reflection of improvements in health and education,

I have never heard
